Thermal Modernization
Location: Raleigh Metro, NC July 31, 2025

10+ Year Aging Tank Replacement with High-Efficiency Tankless System

The Challenge: An old 50-gallon atmospheric gas water heater that had exceeded 11 years of service was struggling to keep up with domestic hot water demands and showing significant sediment accumulation.

The Retrofit: We removed the bulky floor tank, recalculated gas supply requirements, re-routed direct vent lines, and wall-mounted a high-efficiency condensing tankless water heater.

Result: Reclaimed valuable garage floor space, reduced thermal standby energy loss, and provided the household with endless on-demand continuous hot water.

Type: Direct Vent & Tankless Retrofit Endless Hot Water
